## 2024-05-22 - Keyboard Shortcuts in Simulation
**Learning:** In complex simulation interfaces, users expect keyboard shortcuts for frequent actions (like advancing levels or resetting view) to avoid breaking immersion/workflow with mouse clicks.
**Action:** Always map primary HUD actions to single-key shortcuts and expose them via tooltip/visual hint.
